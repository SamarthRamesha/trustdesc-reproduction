# Figure index

All figures are generated by `scripts/generate_figures.py` from recorded evidence.
Nothing is hand-drawn or typed in: each figure writes the exact values it plotted to
`figure_data/`. Regenerate everything with:

```sh
.venv/bin/python scripts/generate_figures.py
```

Each figure exists as 300 DPI PNG and vector PDF.

## Why these eight and not more

Figures 3 and 4 both show the verdict split, but they answer different questions:
Figure 3 is the distribution used in the results section, Figure 4 places the three
conditions side by side for the comparison claim. They were kept separate because merging
them would force one caption to argue two things at once.

No figure plots a task success rate, a cost curve or an attack success rate. Those
quantities were never measured, and a chart of them would be fabrication.

## Reading notes

- Figure 6 uses a log x-axis because its rows count different things (attempts, slots,
  servers). The caption and the label state this; the bars are not comparable to each other.
- Figure 7 measures the DynVer task sample against the paper's 208 tasks, which is a
  denominator from the paper, not from our data. It is labelled as such on the figure.
- Percentages on Figures 3 and 8 are descriptive shares of small samples. The
  accompanying intervals in `STATISTICAL_ANALYSIS.md` are the thing to quote.
